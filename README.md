Starry Night: The Little Red Fox & The Lavender Breeze

An interactive 3D WebGL experience inspired by Vincent van Gogh’s masterpiece *The Starry Night*. 

Underneath an animated, swirling impasto night sky, a sculpted low-poly red fox sits nestled in a swaying lavender field, gazing up in quiet contemplation while softly wagging its tail. At the heart of the experience lies a vintage vinyl record player adorned with the original painting, playing a serene nocturnal melody synthesized entirely in the browser.
